Quick Answers

A few things clients usually ask

What does Obsidian Solutions actually do?

We're six specialists working as one team — covering web & business systems, HR, supply chain analytics, branding & performance marketing, customer service, and social media.

How do I know which specialist I need?

Chat with our assistant below and it'll point you to the right person, or browse the team section and reach out directly — most projects end up touching more than one of us anyway.

Do you work with businesses outside Pakistan?

Yes — most of our work so far has focused on Pakistani brands and payment patterns like Cash on Delivery and WhatsApp ordering, but the same systems adapt well to other markets.

What's the typical timeline for a project?

It depends on scope — a single storefront build usually runs 1–3 weeks, while marketing or HR engagements are often ongoing. We'll scope timeline and cost after a short discovery call.

Can you handle multiple parts of my business at once?

Yes — that's the reason Obsidian Solutions exists. Instead of coordinating six separate freelancers, you work with one team that already talks to each other.

What's the best way to reach the team?

Use the WhatsApp assistant in the corner to get routed to the right specialist, or reach anyone on the team directly from the directory below.
